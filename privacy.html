Nook

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version: Nook has no account, no analytics and no servers of its own. Everything it keeps stays on your computer. It only talks to the services you connect it to, plus GitHub to check for updates.

What we collect

Nothing. The app sends no analytics, usage data or crash reports, and we have no way to see your notes, work log, clipboard, screenshots or settings.

What stays on your computer

  • Notes, work log and settings - in Nook's app data folder.
  • Clipboard history - the last 300 text and image copies plus anything you pin, in the same folder. Copies that apps mark as temporary are skipped; everything else (including passwords you copy) is kept until you delete it or clear the history.
  • Screenshots - kept in Nook's cache folder for a day unless you save them. Text recognition runs on your computer (Apple's Vision on macOS, Tesseract on Linux); nothing is uploaded.
  • Vercel and GitHub tokens - in the macOS Keychain, or your system keyring on Linux.

The app data folder is ~/Library/Application Support/com.waseemdilsher.nook on macOS and ~/.local/share/com.waseemdilsher.nook on Linux. Deleting Nook doesn't remove it - delete that folder too to remove everything.

Network connections

Nook connects only to:

  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com, github.com) - to check for and download updates. No personal information is sent.
  • Vercel (api.vercel.com) and GitHub (api.github.com) - only if you add tokens in the Deploys tab, to show your deployments and workflow runs.
  • Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) and OpenAI (chatgpt.com) - only if you're signed in to Claude Code or Codex on this computer, to read your usage limits for the AI tab. Nook uses the sign-in those tools already have, read-only.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) - to load the app's font.

Your notes, work log, clipboard history and screenshots are never sent anywhere.

Permissions

On macOS, screenshots need Screen Recording permission, which macOS asks for the first time. Notifications are optional. You can change both in System Settings.

This website

This website uses Umami to count page visits. It doesn't use cookies or collect personal information. The app itself has no analytics.

Your control

  • Delete notes, clipboard items and work log entries at any time, or clear the clipboard history.
  • Remove a Vercel or GitHub account in the Deploys tab to delete its token.
  • Use Nook offline - everything except deploys, AI limits and updates works without a connection.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out on GitHub.
